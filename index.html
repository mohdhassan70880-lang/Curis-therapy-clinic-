What are the session charges & consultation fees?

expand_circle_down

We maintain transparent per-session pricing for manual therapy, dry needling, aquatic rehab, and ice bath cryotherapy. Treatment costs vary slightly based on the clinical diagnosis and modalities required. Patients receive customized, discounted multi-session recovery packages. Tap our WhatsApp for an instant fee estimate customized to your condition.

health_and_safety

Is physiotherapy covered under health insurance?

expand_circle_down

Yes, physiotherapy is widely reimbursable under Outpatient (OPD) riders and corporate wellness health plans. CURIS provides official stamped medical invoices, clinical diagnosis sheets, and physiotherapist registration credentials for swift claim submission and paperless reimbursement.

calendar_month

Are there package plans for sports or post-op rehab?

expand_circle_down

Yes, structured 2-week, 4-week, and multi-month rehabilitation programs are available for ACL ligament reconstruction, joint arthroplasty, rotator cuff repair, and athletic return-to-play conditioning at reduced package rates with scheduled progress reviews.
